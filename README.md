## About Me

I am a Statistics undergraduate student at Yogyakarta State University with a strong interest in Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Web Developer. I enjoy transforming data into actionable insights by combining statistical reasoning with modern AI techniques. My experience includes projects in Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing (NLP), such as object detection, image classification, Indonesian NLP, and sentiment analysis. I have worked with TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Hugging Face, while also developing expertise in time series analysis.

Current focus: exploring quantitative finance and financial econometrics, with a particular interest in algorithmic trading strategies, derivative pricing models, risk management, and building data-driven quantitative systems for financial markets.
